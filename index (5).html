Everything an event team juggles

Registration

Share a link and a QR code. People sign up without an account, you approve them, and a waitlist fills empty seats.

QR tickets and check-in

Scan tickets at the gate, even offline. A used ticket shows when and by whom.

Tasks and teams

Give work to people, set due dates, and see everything assigned to you across events.

Roles

Organizers, managers and members. Create custom roles and choose what each may do.

Money

Cash and UPI fees, payment links, bills, vendors, sponsors and a break-even number.

Team chat

A chat inside every event, so the plan stays next to the people running it.
